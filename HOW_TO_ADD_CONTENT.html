📝 Field-by-Field Guide

📌 Title

What it is: The name of your photo or video

Example: "Community Food Distribution 2024"

Tips:

  • Be descriptive and clear
  • Keep it under 60 characters
  • Use proper capitalization

🔗 Slug

What it is: URL-friendly version of your title

How to fill: ALWAYS click the "Generate" button!

Example: "community-food-distribution-2024"

Don't: Type it manually or leave it empty

📄 Description

What it is: Short text about your photo/video

Example: "Volunteers from FFP distributing food packages to families in need during our monthly community outreach program."

Tips:

  • 1-3 sentences is perfect
  • Explain what's happening in the photo
  • Optional but recommended

⚠️ Common Mistakes & Solutions

❌ Mistake #1: Not Clicking "Publish"

Problem: Clicking "Save as draft" instead of "Publish"

Result: Content won't appear on website

Solution: Always click the green "Publish" button!

❌ Mistake #2: Not Generating Slug

Problem: Leaving slug field empty or typing manually

Result: Cannot publish, you'll see error message

Solution: Always click "Generate" button next to slug field!

❌ Mistake #3: Forgetting to Upload Image

Problem: Filling everything except the actual image

Result: Content publishes but has no image to display

Solution: Click "Upload" and select your image file!

❌ Mistake #4: Multiple Active Heroes

Problem: Setting multiple hero sections as "Active"

Result: Only one will show, causes confusion

Solution: Only ONE hero should have "Active" checked at a time!

🆘 Troubleshooting

Can't see the login page?

  • Clear browser cache (Ctrl + Shift + Delete)
  • Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
  • Check your internet connection
  • Verify URL: https://ffp-dashboard.sanity.studio/

Can't click "Publish" button?

  • Make sure all required fields (✱) are filled
  • Slug must be generated
  • Title cannot be empty
  • Check for error messages in red

Image won't upload?

  • Check file size (try under 5 MB)
  • Use common formats (JPG, PNG)
  • Check internet connection
  • Wait for upload to complete (don't click away)
  • Try a different image to test

Content published but not showing on website?

  • This is normal if website not deployed yet!
  • Upload FFP-WEBSITE-CPANEL.zip to cPanel first
  • Content will appear after website deployment
  • If deployed: Clear browser cache (Ctrl + Shift + R)
