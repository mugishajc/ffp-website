## 🎯 Let's Add Your First Photo (Step by Step)

### Step 1: Open Your Studio

1. **Open your browser** (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge)
2. **Go to**: https://ffp-dashboard.sanity.studio/
3. **You should see**: Sanity login page

### Step 2: Log In

1. Click **"Sign in with Sanity.io"** button
2. Choose your login method (Google recommended)
3. Log in with your Sanity account
4. **You'll see**: The Studio dashboard

### Step 3: Navigate to Media Gallery

**What you'll see on the left sidebar:**
```
🎨 Structure
  - Media Gallery
  - News & Updates
  - Team Members
  - Hero Section
```

1. **Click on "Media Gallery"** in the left sidebar
2. **You'll see**: An empty list (since no content yet)

### Step 4: Create New Media Item

1. Look at the **top right corner**
2. **Click the "+ Create" button** (green button)
3. **You'll see**: A blank form with fields

### Step 5: Fill in the Form

Here's what each field means and what to enter:

#### 📝 **Title** (Required - Red asterisk)
- **What it is**: Name of your photo/video
- **Example**: "Community Event 2024" or "Children Receiving Meals"
- **Type**: Just text

#### 🔗 **Slug** (Required - Red asterisk)
- **What it is**: URL-friendly version of title
- **How to fill**: DON'T type manually!
  1. Click the **"Generate"** button next to the slug field
  2. It auto-creates from your title
- **Example**: If title is "Community Event 2024", slug becomes "community-event-2024"

#### 📄 **Description** (Optional)
- **What it is**: Short description of the photo
- **Example**: "Volunteers distributing food packages to families"
- **Type**: A few sentences

#### 📷 **Media Type** (Required - Red asterisk)
- **What it is**: Choose Photo or Video
- **You'll see**: Two radio buttons
  - ⭕ Photo
  - ⭕ Video
- **Select**: Click "Photo" for now

#### 🖼️ **Image** (Shows up after selecting "Photo")
- **What it is**: Your actual photo
- **How to upload**:
  1. Click the **"Upload"** button or drag & drop area
  2. Your file explorer opens
  3. Select a photo from your computer
  4. Click "Open"
  5. Wait for upload (you'll see progress bar)
  6. ✅ Image appears in the form

#### 🏷️ **Category** (Optional)
- **What it is**: Organize your media
- **Options**:
  - Events
  - Programs
  - Community
  - Impact
  - News
- **Select**: Click dropdown and choose one

#### ⭐ **Featured** (Optional)
- **What it is**: Show on homepage
- **You'll see**: A checkbox
- **Check it** if you want this photo on your homepage
- **Leave unchecked** for regular gallery photos

#### 📅 **Published At** (Auto-filled)
- **What it is**: Date/time of publication
- **Already filled**: Today's date
- **You can**: Change if needed, or leave as is

### Step 6: Publish Your Content

**VERY IMPORTANT:**

1. Look at the **bottom right corner**
2. You'll see **two buttons**:
   - "Save as draft" (gray)
   - "Publish" (green)

3. **Click "Publish"** button (the green one)

**Why "Publish" not "Save as draft"?**
- ✅ "Publish" = Content goes live on your website
- ❌ "Save as draft" = Content stays hidden, won't show on site

4. After clicking Publish:
   - Button changes to "Published"
   - Green checkmark appears
   - **Your content is now LIVE!** 🎉

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake 1: Not Clicking "Publish"
**Problem**: Clicking "Save as draft" instead of "Publish"
**Result**: Content won't appear on website
**Solution**: Always click green "Publish" button!

### ❌ Mistake 2: Not Generating Slug
**Problem**: Leaving slug field empty
**Result**: Can't publish, you'll see error
**Solution**: Always click "Generate" button for slug!

### ❌ Mistake 3: Not Uploading Image
**Problem**: Filling everything except the actual image
**Result**: Content publishes but has no image
**Solution**: Click "Upload" and select your image file!

### ❌ Mistake 4: Multiple Active Heroes
**Problem**: Setting multiple heroes as "Active"
**Result**: Only one will show, may cause confusion
**Solution**: Only one hero should have "Active" checked!

## 🆘 Still Stuck?

### Can't see the login page?
- Clear browser cache (Ctrl + Shift + Delete)
- Try different browser
- Check internet connection

### Can't click "Publish"?
- Make sure all required fields (red asterisk *) are filled
- Slug must be generated
- Title must not be empty

### Image won't upload?
- Check image size (max 10 MB usually works)
- Try JPG or PNG format
- Check internet connection
- Wait for upload to complete

### Content published but not showing?
- This is normal! Your website needs to be deployed first
- Upload `ffp-cpanel-deployment.zip` to cPanel
- Content will appear after deployment
